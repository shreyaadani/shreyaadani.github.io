About

The mind behind the metrics.

I'm finishing my Master's in Computer Science at UMass Amherst — but the real education started at IBM at 21, deep in enterprise backend systems, learning what production means when 500 people depend on your code not breaking on a Tuesday at 3am.

Then Chewy handed me a blank canvas and a hard problem. I didn't build just one thing — I built the OCR pipeline, the LLM extraction layer, the validation engine, the clinical knowledge graph in AWS Neptune, the approval prediction model, and the veterinary chatbot.

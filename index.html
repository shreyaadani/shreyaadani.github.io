About

The mind behind the metrics.

I'm finishing my Master's in Computer Science at UMass Amherst—but the real education started at IBM at 21, deep in enterprise backend systems, learning what production means when 500 people depend on your code not breaking.

Then Chewy gave me a blank canvas and a hard problem: veterinarians drowning in prescription reviews. I built the full stack—OCR pipeline, LLM extraction, validation engine, knowledge graph (AWS Neptune), approval prediction model, and a clinical chatbot. $1.4M saved annually. 92% user satisfaction. Runner-up at Chewy's Innovator's Arena. And they brought me back for a second internship.

Now I'm a Research Extern in collaboration with Cisco under Prof. Andrew McCallum, studying adaptive monitoring for LLM agents. If you're deploying AI in critical systems, someone should verify it's doing what you think it is.

I also represent 400+ CS graduate students as a GSS Senator for Computer Science at UMass Amherst, and TA a graduate-level optimization course. Outside the IDE? Art, music, anime, way too much TikTok.
